NeusktchmechArc: Artistic Fiction Declaration, Origin, Nature, and Internal Structure
The artistic framework within NeusktchmechArc is my personal art archive, a fictional and internally constructed reference space through which I describe how lived impressions gradually take visual form. It functions as a narrative language rather than as an instructional or operational construct. The term “archive” is used here in a descriptive sense to indicate continuity of reference, not institutional storage or cataloguing.
NeusktchmechArc artistic framework is not a system, method, programme, or professional model. It does not teach, instruct, diagnose, guide, or advise. It exists solely as a descriptive vocabulary through which I articulate how visual ideas emerge, pause, shift, and stabilise within my creative awareness. This text does not prescribe how art should be made, nor does it propose a transferable method of creation; it records how this practice is internally understood and articulated by its originator.
This framework exists to provide context rather than interpretation. It allows viewers or curators to understand the internal coherence underlying my work without assigning fixed meaning or directing interpretation.
The NeusktchmechArc artistic framework is an artistic work of fiction. It is a self-authored internal narrative record shaped through long-term private writing, observation of everyday environments and human behaviour, cross-cultural exposure, and sustained visual lived experience. Nothing within this framework claims scientific, therapeutic, psychological, educational, or academic authority. All terminology is self-defined and used exclusively within this conceptual context, and any resemblance to existing concepts, metaphors, or creative practices is unintended and coincidental. The narrative voice, terminology, and internal vocabulary used throughout this document - including Interior Atlas, First Emanation, Tranquil Rift, Integration Phase, and related descriptive groupings are self-authored artistic constructs and should not be read as factual claims. Where this document refers to lived professional or biographical context, such references are factual in nature and are provided solely as contextual grounding for the creative practice. They are addressed further, and governed, by the Founder Context & Professional Boundary Disclaimer and the Disclaimers & Legal Context accompanying this website.
Any reference to a potential core structure of NeusktchmechArc reflects a conceptual vision only. Should such a structure be articulated in the future, it would be introduced solely at the discretion of the creator. Until that time, no fixed framework, obligation, operational model, or enforceable structure is asserted or implied. The artistic framework remains a creative and fictional construct, and no legal, professional, institutional, or commercial commitments arise from its current description.
Methodological Status
This text does not function as theory in the academic sense, nor as method in the procedural sense. It is best understood as a first-person descriptive articulation of practice, written to preserve internal coherence rather than to invite replication or critique.
Influences & Context
The NeusktchmechArc artistic framework has been independently developed in its internal structure, while having developed through long-term reflective writing, sustained self-observation, cross-cultural exposure, years of community engagement and educational involvement, and an ongoing interest in how internal experience takes visual form. It is articulated as an artistic orientation rather than as a technical system, method, or operational construct.
At present, NeusktchmechArc remains grounded exclusively in my own lived experience and internal observation. It is not formally linked to any external theory, movement, discipline, or school of thought. If, at a later stage, there is value in situating this creative archive in loose relation to broader artistic or conceptual traditions, this may be addressed in a separate and clearly identified contextual note. Any such note would exist outside the core body of this work and would be presented solely for contextual reference, not as a source, influence, or explanatory foundation for the concepts described here.

On the Naming of NeusktchmechArc
I named the archive NeusktchmechArc as a single coined word, not as a constructed acronym. Its form emerged in much the same way as the works within this practice: as an initial resonance given shape before it was fully explained.
"Arc" is the one element I have chosen to make immediately legible. It represents the movement from interior impression to external articulation, the progression traced throughout the archive through the Interior Atlas, First Emanation, Tranquil Rift, and Integration Phase. The remainder of the name stays close to the intuitive manner in which it first arrived. Rather than being analytically assembled, it gradually acquires meaning through the artworks, poetry, language, and evolving structure of the archive.
This naming instinct did not arise in isolation. Years spent across academic, healthcare domain, corporate industry, and research settings cultivated a lasting curiosity about how internal states, experiences, and perceptions move toward outward expression. Within this practice, poetry often becomes an early verbal form of the same inner experience that may 
later emerge through visual art. NeusktchmechArc is a personal continuation of that curiosity. Its artistic framework represents one distinct expression of this broader intellectual and creative interest, while scholarly and professional activities, where documented through this personal archive, remain separately identified and contextualised.
